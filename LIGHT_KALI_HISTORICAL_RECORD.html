English

On September 2, 2026, Light Kali Web OS was publicly presented as a Web OS project developed in Iran. The creator publicly identifies it as the “first Web OS from Iran.” This record preserves the creator’s public historical claim and the project references available on the record date. It is not, by itself, a governmental registration, patent registration, trademark registration, or independent legal determination of ownership or priority.
